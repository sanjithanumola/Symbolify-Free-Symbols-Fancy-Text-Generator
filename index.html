About Symbolify

Symbolify is a free, modern web utility inspired by CoolSymbol, designed to help creators, gamers, writers, and social media enthusiasts find, copy, and customize special Unicode symbols, fancy text styles, aesthetic characters, emojis, and kaomojis in seconds.

How It Works

Unicode is the international standard covering over 149,000 characters from different alphabets, mathematical domains, and historical scripts. Symbolify organizes these characters into intuitive categories and transforms plain alphabet characters into mathematical alphanumeric symbols so you can copy and paste them onto Instagram, Discord, TikTok, Roblox, Twitter/X, and WhatsApp.

Privacy & Offline Support

Symbolify is 100% client-side. No tracking scripts, no external databases, and no user accounts required. Your starred favorites, recent clipboard history, and theme settings are safely stored in your own browser using standard localStorage.

Device Compatibility

Symbols and emojis render through your system's native Unicode fonts. Most modern operating systems (iOS 15+, Android 10+, Windows 10/11, macOS) support the vast majority of characters. Some older devices may show missing glyph boxes (□) for rare historic or mathematical codepoints.
